# CEO Monthly Routines

The CEO's planned monthly operating cadence, once active in a future
phase. This is where the longer-horizon views — `COMPANY_HEALTH_MODEL.md`
and `STRATEGIC_PLANNING.md` — are exercised, as distinct from the weekly
priority-ordering cadence in `WEEKLY_ROUTINES.md`.

## The Monthly Cross-Domain Review

Per `EXECUTIVE_MEETING_GUIDE.md`'s "Monthly Cross-Domain Check-In"
section: assemble each sibling executive's own `*/MONTHLY_ROUTINES.md`
output (CTO technical-debt/risk trend, COO operational-health trend,
CFO monthly revenue/subscription/cash-flow close per
`cfo/MONTHLY_ROUTINES.md`, CMO monthly growth/campaign performance, CPO
monthly product-health trend) into the `COMPANY_HEALTH_MODEL.md`
five-dimension view.

## Steps

1. Pull each domain's own monthly-cadence output — never recompute a
   domain's own numbers independently of that domain's own
   documentation.
2. Update the side-by-side view in `COMPANY_HEALTH_MODEL.md`, flagging
   any dimension where the underlying domain executive itself reports
   "not tracked" rather than filling the gap.
3. Re-check `STRATEGIC_PLANNING.md`'s cross-domain dependency map
   against the month's actual developments — has a CTO roadmap item
   (e.g. the house-number/customization persistence gap) moved, and
   does that change a COO or CFO consequence previously noted?
4. Re-check `ROADMAP.md` (the CEO's own readiness roadmap) — has
   anything changed about whether `ai/core/` or `ai/integrations/`
   exist yet?

## What This Cadence Is Not

- Not a board meeting or investor update — that's the CFO's domain
  (`cfo/INVESTOR_REPORTING.md`), which the CEO may synthesize alongside
  other domains but does not own or draft independently.
- Not a strategic-planning session that sets new direction — it
  reviews and synthesizes what already exists across the five domains'
  own documentation; setting new strategy remains the founder's call,
  informed by this synthesis.

## Current Status

As of Phase 8, none of this runs — no domain executive is reading live
monthly data yet (`ai/integrations/` doesn't exist), and this document
specifies the intended cadence for once that layer exists.
