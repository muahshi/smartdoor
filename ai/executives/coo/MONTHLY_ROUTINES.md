# COO Monthly Routines

Shape: see `ai/core/standards/MEETING_STANDARD.md`.
The COO's planned monthly operating cadence, once active in a future
phase. Additive to, not a replacement for, `OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md` §3
(Monthly) — infra-flavored items (API key rotation, Sentry/Clarity UX
review) remain the CTO's.

## Monthly Checklist (Future Phase — Not Executed Today)

- [ ] Review month-over-month manufacturing and shipping SLA adherence
  against `docs/legal/shipping-policy.md` and the Manufacturing Checklist
  referenced in `SUPPORT_RUNBOOK.md` §3.4.
- [ ] Review inventory batch turnover and any recurring batch-level
  defect patterns flagged during the month (`INVENTORY_GUIDE.md`,
  `MANUFACTURING_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review installation-to-activation conversion — how many delivered
  plates remain un-activated past a reasonable window
  (`INSTALLATION_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review support ticket category trends month-over-month to spot a
  slow-building systemic issue that wouldn't show up in a single week's
  data.
- [ ] Review partner/dealer operational health (onboarding turnaround,
  KYC approval rate) as a distinct concern from partner commission
  math, which remains CFO territory.
- [ ] Cross-check the Company Brain (`ai/knowledge/workflows/workflows.md`,
  `ai/knowledge/business/business_rules.md`) against anything observed
  operationally this month that isn't reflected there — flag drift per
  `ai/docs/COMPANY_BRAIN.md`, don't silently patch it.

## What This Routine Is Not

- Not an automated job. As of Phase 3, no runtime exists to execute this
  routine.
- Not a substitute for the founder's own monthly business review.
