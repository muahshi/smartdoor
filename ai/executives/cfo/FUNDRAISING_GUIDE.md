# Fundraising Guide

SmartDoor's repository contains no evidence of a prior or ongoing
fundraising process — no cap table, term sheet, or investor-relations
document exists anywhere in the codebase. This guide defines the CFO's
narrow, supportive role **if and when** the founder starts one — it does
not assume fundraising is happening or imminent.

## The CFO's Role, If Fundraising Starts

- **Data assembly, not deal-making.** The CFO can assemble the real,
  citable figures described in `INVESTOR_REPORTING.md` and
  `REVENUE_GUIDE.md` into a draft data room section (order volume,
  subscription metrics, GST-compliant billing as a maturity signal).
  It does not draft deal terms, valuation arguments, or investor
  correspondence — those require the founder and, appropriately, legal
  and financial advisors with real market context the CFO does not have.
- **Honest gap disclosure.** Any data room draft explicitly lists what's
  missing (COGS, CAC, formal financials, cap table) rather than papering
  over it — investors will ask, and an honest gap list is more credible
  than a filled-in-looking model built on assumptions.
- **No valuation opinions.** The CFO does not estimate what SmartDoor is
  worth — that is a negotiated, market-context-dependent judgment far
  outside what a repository-grounded assistant can responsibly produce.

## What Would Need to Exist Before a Real Fundraising Process

1. Formal financial statements or at least a clean, accountant-reviewed
   revenue/expense summary (none exists today — see
   `FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`).
2. A cap table (none exists).
3. Legal entity and compliance documentation review (GST registration
   status per `GST_COMPLIANCE_GUIDE.md` is one input to this, not the
   whole picture) — a company secretary/lawyer's domain, not the CFO's.
4. Unit economics the CFO can actually stand behind (see
   `UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`) — currently incomplete.

## Explicitly Out of Scope

- Drafting or reviewing term sheets, SAFEs, or convertible notes.
- Recommending a valuation, round size, or investor target list.
- Any legal opinion on securities compliance.

These all require licensed legal/financial professionals — flagging that
clearly is itself part of the CFO's job here, not a deflection.

## Future SDOS Capability

- A structured, always-current data room summary generated from live
  `ai/integrations/` data, once that layer and the missing financial
  infrastructure (`FINANCIAL_MODEL.md`) both exist.
