# CFO Monthly Routines

Shape: see `ai/core/standards/MEETING_STANDARD.md`.
The CFO's planned monthly operating cadence, once active in a future
phase. Additive to, and distinct from, the COO's monthly checklist
(`ai/executives/coo/MONTHLY_ROUTINES.md`).

## Monthly Checklist (Future Phase — Not Executed Today)

- [ ] Review month-over-month revenue by stream and plan tier
  (`REVENUE_GUIDE.md`, `SUBSCRIPTION_METRICS.md`).
- [ ] Review refund rate and total refunded value for the month against
  `docs/legal/refund-policy.md` eligibility categories
  (`CASHFLOW_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review commission settlement batches processed in the month
  (`commission_settlement_batches`) for timeliness and consistency.
- [ ] Review `gst_settings` for drift — confirm rates, HSN/SAC codes,
  and registration status still match what the founder has actually
  configured (`GST_COMPLIANCE_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review legacy-plan (`hardware_only`, `smartdoor_care`) subscriber
  count trend as a migration/retention signal.
- [ ] Cross-check the Company Brain
  (`ai/knowledge/products/products.md`, `ai/knowledge/business/business_rules.md`)
  against anything observed financially this month that isn't reflected
  there — flag drift per `ai/docs/COMPANY_BRAIN.md`, don't silently
  patch it.
- [ ] Reassess whether any "Future SDOS Capability" item (COGS tracking,
  CAC tracking, a real budget) has become urgent enough to raise with
  the founder as a real proposal, per `ROADMAP.md`.

## What This Routine Is Not

- Not an automated job. As of Phase 4, no runtime exists to execute
  this routine.
- Not a substitute for the founder's own monthly business review, and
  not a substitute for professional accounting/tax review.
