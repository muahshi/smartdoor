# CMO Monthly Routines

Shape: see `ai/core/standards/MEETING_STANDARD.md`.
The CMO's planned monthly operating cadence, once active in a future
phase. Additive to, and distinct from, the COO's and CFO's monthly
checklists.

## Monthly Checklist (Future Phase — Not Executed Today)

- [ ] Review month-over-month `customer_segment_breakdown_view` for
  segment mix shift (`beta` / `early_access` / `paying` / `vip`) as a
  growth-stage signal (`ANALYTICS_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review `churn_analysis_view` for the month alongside
  `pmf_metrics_view`'s retention/renewal rates — flag any deterioration
  jointly with the COO (operational root cause) and CFO (revenue
  impact), per `INTER_EXECUTIVE_COMMUNICATION.md`.
- [ ] Review any campaign that ended during the month
  (`campaigns_with_status`) — draft a founder-facing summary of what
  ran, honest that redemption-to-revenue linkage cannot currently be
  computed (`CAMPAIGN_GUIDE.md`, `ANALYTICS_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Reassess `robots.txt`'s GEO/AEO crawler allow-list and `llms.txt`
  against current AI-answer-engine landscape norms — flag to the
  founder/CTO if a now-common crawler is missing (`SEO_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Cross-check the Company Brain (`ai/knowledge/company/company_profile.md`,
  `ai/knowledge/products/products.md`) against anything observed this
  month that isn't reflected there — flag drift per
  `ai/docs/COMPANY_BRAIN.md`, don't silently patch it.
- [ ] Reassess whether any "Future SDOS Capability" item (attribution
  tracking, a CMS, an ad-platform integration) has become urgent enough
  to raise with the founder as a real proposal, per `ROADMAP.md`.

## What This Routine Is Not

- Not an automated job. As of Phase 6, no runtime exists to execute
  this routine.
- Not a substitute for the founder's own monthly business review, and
  not a substitute for professional marketing/legal review of any
  externally-published claim.
