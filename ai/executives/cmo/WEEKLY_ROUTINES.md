# CMO Weekly Routines

Shape: see `ai/core/standards/MEETING_STANDARD.md`.
The CMO's planned weekly operating cadence, once active in a future
phase. Additive to, and distinct from, the COO's and CFO's weekly
checklists.

## Weekly Checklist (Future Phase — Not Executed Today)

- [ ] Review `pmf_metrics_view`'s `avg_referral_intent` and
  `avg_renewal_intent` trend for the week (`ANALYTICS_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review the referral leaderboard (`getReferralLeaderboard()`) for
  the week — any new top referrers worth founder-approved public
  recognition (`LEAD_GENERATION_GUIDE.md`).
- [ ] Review `getReviewsSummary()` for the week's average ratings across
  `product_rating` / `manufacturing_rating` / `delivery_rating` — flag
  any negative trend to the COO (manufacturing/delivery root cause is
  their domain) rather than treating it as purely a marketing signal.
- [ ] Cross-check any campaign active during the week
  (`campaigns_with_status`) against its `pricing_rules` for unexpected
  stacking behavior — coordinate with the CFO per
  `INTER_EXECUTIVE_COMMUNICATION.md` if found.
- [ ] Spot-check `sitemap.xml` and `llms.txt` against the live page
  inventory in `ai/knowledge/pages/pages.md` for drift
  (`SEO_GUIDE.md`).

## What This Routine Is Not

- Not an automated job. As of Phase 6, no runtime exists to execute
  this routine.
- Not a substitute for the founder reviewing anything that surfaces a
  founder-approval-required item per `AUTHORITY_MATRIX.md`.
